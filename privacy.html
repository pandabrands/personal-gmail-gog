Privacy policy

Personal Gmail for gog is a personal integration operated by Adam Bunch. This policy describes how the integration handles Google user data.

Data accessed

With the account owner’s consent, the integration can read Gmail messages and associated metadata, including senders, recipients, subjects, message content and settings available through the granted read permission. It can send email from the authorised account. Google also provides account identity information, including the email address.

How data is used

Data is used to carry out tasks requested by the account owner, including searching and summarising messages, locating sign-in codes and sending approved emails. Gmail access is used only for these user-facing functions.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are kept in gog’s local credential storage. Retrieved email content may be processed in memory and may also appear in the selected assistant’s chat history or in files the owner requests. Retention depends on the owner’s settings and the tools used for the task. The account owner controls access to the computers and local files used by the integration.

Sharing and AI processing

When the owner requests assistance with email, relevant message content may be supplied to the owner’s configured AI assistant or other tool needed to complete that task. Those providers process data under their applicable terms and account data controls. Email is disclosed to recipients when the owner authorises sending. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. This integration does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Google Limited Use

The integration’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoke access or request deletion

The owner can revoke access in Google Account connections and remove the account’s stored credential from gog. Revoking access prevents future API access. Any retained local files or assistant conversations can be deleted through the tools where they are stored. For a privacy or deletion request, contact adambunch82@gmail.com.

This website

This static information site does not request Gmail access or collect email content. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
