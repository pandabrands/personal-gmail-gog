Personal Gmail for gog

A personal Gmail integration operated by Adam Bunch. It connects an authorised Google account to the gog command-line tool for finding and reading emails, retrieving sign-in codes, and sending emails when requested by the account owner.

The integration requests Gmail read access and permission to send email. Google handles account sign-in and consent. Access can be removed in Google Account connections.

This app is for the owner’s personal use. Its website provides app and privacy information.

Read the privacy policy for details of access, use, storage, sharing and removal.
